## What is preserved

The true widescreen geometry; white canvas; blue/slate heading hierarchy;
navy subject titles; restrained purple/teal accents; pale rounded panels;
three-column and six-card grids; stacked architecture; native table styling;
small evidence line; breadcrumb, brand zone, and page-number footer.

The recurring content roles also inform the templates: conclusion, scope,
evidence, caveat, proposed approach, ownership, acceptance, and next decision.
Those roles may be adapted to another topic without copying the original claims.

## What is not inherited

The original 20-slide count, organization name, cover illustration, product
names, research dates, pricing, source IDs, task codes, numeric thresholds,
proposed commitments, or hidden speaker-note research. The starter is a new,
source-neutral presentation rather than a redacted copy of the original package.

The original is not redistributed inside this kit. The kit does not include
font binaries. All sample slide wording is instructional or placeholder text.

## Source limitations and deliberate distinctions

Explicit Arial font declarations do not establish a single exact Chinese
rendering face. The source table has inconsistent XML frame/grid dimensions.
Some original small boxes allow awkward line breaks. These are documented
limitations, not desirable style traits to reproduce.

The source does not demonstrate a chart or animation system. New chart styles,
projection-friendly enlarged text, or other additional families should be
identified as compatible extensions rather than source-extracted facts.
